## SLIDE 4 — THE SOLUTION

# BlockFinaX — Peer-to-peer parametric protection for African businesses.

**One sentence:** If the market moves against you past your chosen level, you get paid. Automatically.

**How it works:**
1. A business selects the currency pair they are exposed to
2. They set the price level they are afraid of
3. They pay a small premium — as low as 2.5% of coverage
4. If the market touches their level, payout is automatic and instant
5. No bank. No broker. No paperwork.

**Built on blockchain** — every settlement is public, permanent, and tamper-proof.
